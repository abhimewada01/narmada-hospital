Who We Are

About Maa Narmada Multi Speciality Hospital

Trusted Healthcare Partner in Sehore

Narmada Hospital (Maa Narmada Multi Speciality Hospital) is a premier healthcare institution located in Sehore, Madhya Pradesh. Dedicated to patient-centric care, our hospital brings together skilled medical specialists, modern diagnostic equipment, and state-of-the-art facilities under one roof.

Our objective is to deliver accessible, affordable, and high-quality medical services to individuals and families in our community. Whether it is routine clinical consultations, surgical interventions, or round-the-clock emergency medical response, we are committed to keeping your health first.

Our Specialities

Comprehensive medical care tailored to your needs with expert attention and advanced technology.

CTVS (Cardio Thoracic & Vascular Surgery)

Advanced surgical care for heart, chest, and vascular diseases including bypass surgery, valve replacement, congenital heart surgery, vascular procedures, and minimally invasive cardiac surgeries performed by experienced specialists.

Cardiology & Heart Care

Comprehensive diagnosis and treatment for heart diseases including hypertension, chest pain, heart failure, arrhythmia, angiography, angioplasty, and preventive cardiac care.

Oncology & Cancer Care

Comprehensive cancer diagnosis, treatment, and supportive care with advanced medical technology and personalized treatment plans for various types of cancers.

General Medicine

Expert treatment for fever, infections, diabetes, thyroid disorders, blood pressure, and other medical conditions.

Emergency & Trauma Care

Round-the-clock emergency services with rapid response and expert medical assistance.

Orthopaedics

Specialized care for bone, joint, spine, arthritis, fractures, and sports injuries.

Gastroenterology

Expert care for digestive system disorders including liver, stomach, pancreas, and intestinal diseases.

Radiology & Diagnostics

Modern diagnostic services including ECG, 2D Echo, Ultrasound, X-Ray, CT Scan, pathology, and laboratory services.

Neonatal & Paediatric Care

Specialized healthcare services for newborns, infants, children, and adolescents including neonatal intensive care, vaccination, growth monitoring, and treatment for paediatric illnesses.

Critical Care & ICU

Advanced intensive care facilities with 24x7 monitoring and emergency support for critically ill patients.

General & Laparoscopic Surgery

Advanced surgical procedures using modern minimally invasive techniques for faster recovery and better outcomes.

Neurology

Diagnosis and treatment for neurological disorders including stroke, migraine, epilepsy, and nerve-related conditions.

Pulmonology & Respiratory Care

Treatment for asthma, COPD, lung infections, and respiratory disorders.
